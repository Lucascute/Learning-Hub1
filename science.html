🧪 Science 10 Notes

A simple and easy-to-review collection of Science 10 notes. Use these notes to review important concepts, terms, and ideas.

🔬 Lesson 1: Introduction to Science

What is Science?

Science is the systematic study of the natural world through observation, experimentation, measurement, and analysis.

Scientific Method

  1. Ask a question.
  2. Make an observation.
  3. Form a hypothesis.
  4. Conduct an experiment.
  5. Collect and analyze data.
  6. Draw a conclusion.
⭐ Remember:

Science uses evidence and logical reasoning to understand how the natural world works.

🧬 Lesson 2: Cells and Their Functions

Cell

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.

Nucleus

Controls many activities of the cell.

Mitochondria

Produces energy for the cell.

Cell Membrane

Controls what enters and leaves the cell.

Cytoplasm

Jelly-like substance where many cell activities occur.

⭐ Remember:

Cells work together to perform the functions necessary for life.

🧬 Lesson 3: Genetics and Heredity

Heredity

Heredity is the passing of characteristics from parents to offspring.

Gene

A unit of heredity that influences a trait.

DNA

Contains genetic information of an organism.

Trait

A characteristic of an organism.

Allele

A different form of a gene.

⭐ Remember:

Genetic information is passed from parents to their offspring.

🌱 Lesson 4: Evolution

Evolution

Evolution refers to changes in inherited characteristics of populations over generations.

Natural Selection

Natural selection is the process in which organisms with characteristics that help them survive and reproduce are more likely to pass those traits to the next generation.

⭐ Remember:

Organisms with advantageous adaptations are more likely to survive and reproduce.

⚛️ Lesson 5: Matter and Its Properties

Matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.

Solid

Has a definite shape and volume.

Liquid

Has definite volume but takes the shape of its container.

Gas

Has no definite shape or volume.

Physical Change

Changes the form or appearance of matter.

⭐ Remember:

A physical change does not create a completely new substance.

🧪 Lesson 6: Chemical Reactions

Chemical Reaction

A chemical reaction occurs when substances interact and form new substances.

Common Signs

  • Change in color
  • Formation of gas
  • Formation of a precipitate
  • Change in temperature
  • Production of light
⭐ Remember:

Chemical changes produce substances with different properties from the original substances.

⚡ Lesson 7: Electricity

Electric Current

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor.

Voltage

The electrical potential difference that drives current.

Current

The flow of electric charge.

Resistance

Opposition to the flow of electric current.

Conductor

A material that allows electric current to flow easily.

⭐ Remember:

A complete circuit is needed for electric current to continuously flow.

🌍 Lesson 8: Earth and Its Resources

Natural Resources

Natural resources are materials and substances obtained from nature and used by humans.

Examples

  • Water
  • Soil
  • Minerals
  • Forests
  • Fossil fuels
⭐ Remember:

Natural resources should be used responsibly to protect the environment and future generations.

🌦️ Lesson 9: Weather and Climate

Weather

Weather refers to the short-term condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time.

Climate

Climate refers to the long-term pattern of weather conditions in a particular region.

⭐ Remember:

Weather is short-term, while climate describes long-term patterns.

🌎 Lesson 10: Environmental Issues

Environmental Problems

  • Climate change
  • Air pollution
  • Water pollution
  • Deforestation
  • Loss of biodiversity
  • Improper waste disposal

What Can We Do?

  • Reduce waste.
  • Reuse materials.
  • Recycle whenever possible.
  • Save water and electricity.
  • Protect forests and wildlife.
  • Keep the environment clean.
⭐ Remember:

Protecting the environment is a shared responsibility. Small actions can contribute to a healthier planet.
