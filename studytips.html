💡 Study Tips

Simple tips to make studying easier and more effective.

1

⏰ Manage Your Time

Create a study schedule and set specific times for each subject. Avoid studying everything at the last minute.

2

📝 Take Good Notes

Write down important ideas, definitions, examples, and key information while studying.

3

📵 Avoid Distractions

Put away unnecessary gadgets and choose a quiet place where you can focus on your studies.

4

🔁 Review Regularly

Review your lessons regularly instead of waiting until the night before an examination.

5

😴 Get Enough Sleep

Rest is important for learning. Make sure you get enough sleep so you can stay focused.
